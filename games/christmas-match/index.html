About the game

Christmas is here, and it brought its own memory card game! Two ways to play:

  • Quick Game: pick a board size and match all the pairs at your own pace.
  • Challenge: 24 levels with a limited number of taps. Every 2 levels it gets harder, all the way up to a 72-card board. Can you beat them all?

Cozy winter scenery, falling snow and festive music. Play in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German or Portuguese.

Christmas Match is free to play and supported by ads. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase in Options removes the banner and the ads between levels for good. No account needed.
