About the game

The fastest and cutest one-button game.

Crokie is a frog who loves a challenge. Help her dodge as many logs as you can while you catch the items that let you change Crokie's look and buy new power-ups.

It doesn't look that hard... but the river speeds up very fast, and only 2% of our test players got past 90 logs in the red level.

Two difficulty levels:

  • Green: it gets tricky soon, and scoring more than 100 won't be easy.
  • Red: score 80 in the green level to unlock it and face THE TRUE CHALLENGE.

Collect apples, nuts, bones and crystals to:

  • Unlock new frogs: robot, skeleton and alien.
  • Dress Crokie up with masks, moustaches, hats, gloves and shoes.
  • Buy power-ups: magnet, laser, ghost and the slow-motion flux capacitor.

Watch an optional video to open a chest full of items.

Play in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German or Portuguese.
