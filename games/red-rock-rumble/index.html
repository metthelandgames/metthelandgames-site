About the game

Today's forecast: a one hundred percent chance of asteroids. And you're the rookie on duty.

General Buck Granite has just promoted you to the experimental Mars Defense Astro Cannon. It has never been fired. Ma Sprocket is still bolting it together, and Sunny Vega is reading the apocalypse like a weather report. Don't let a single rock touch the planet.

  • Skill-based arcade action: turn with buttons or tap to aim, in portrait or landscape
  • Charge your specials and blast everything with them
  • After each wave, pick an upgrade: rapid fire, homing missiles, moons, shields, satellites and more
  • Mars doesn't heal on its own, so every pick is a choice between firepower and survival
  • Beat four bosses to complete a loop and face harder waves
  • Ma's workshop turns your scrap into permanent blueprints for future runs
  • A comic-style story with a cast of lovable lunatics
  • A surf-rock and theremin soundtrack
  • Play offline, in six languages
