About the game

Your herd escaped to Earth. Get them back before anyone notices!

Fly your little saucer over the world's most famous places and beam up the runaway critters, colour by colour, in the order your cargo manifest asks for. Every level you clear cracks open a hand-painted postcard of the destination. When the last shard falls, you discover the monument, where it is and a little story about it.

  • 68 destinations, from the Eiffel Tower to Machu Picchu, each with its own painting
  • 680 quick levels with a gentle difficulty ramp, ten per destination
  • New surprises along the way: wanderers, sleepyheads, drifting clouds, big critters, night shifts, tall grass and runaways
  • Every place has its own ground: city cobbles, desert sand, alpine snow, salt flats…
  • A globe that fills up with pins as you travel
  • Replay any level to beat your best time
  • Play offline: no energy, no lives, no waiting
  • One-finger controls, squishy jelly critters and relaxing music
