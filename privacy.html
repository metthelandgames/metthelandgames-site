Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information met the land games ("we") collects when you play our games, why, and the choices you have. It applies to all games published by met the land games on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Who we are

met the land games is run by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: metthelandgames@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Advertising data. Our games show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads. With your consent, ads can be personalized.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your payment details, only a receipt confirming the purchase.
  • Game services. If you sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, we receive your player ID and display name to provide leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves.
  • Diagnostics and gameplay analytics. Anonymous crash reports and gameplay events (for example, levels completed) help us fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your consent and choices

When required (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), our games ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. On iOS, we ask for permission before tracking under App Tracking Transparency. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google Play / Google Play Games Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Game Center: Apple Privacy Policy

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, email metthelandgames@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
